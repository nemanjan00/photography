**Belgrade**, 25 October 2026 — the night SNS fell.

Two years after the Novi Sad station canopy collapsed and killed 16 people,
the students who took to the streets over it won at the ballot box. Turnout
was the highest since 1992, the Student List was projected a majority, and
Aleksandar Vučić conceded that same night.

SNS, in power since 2012, was Serbia's return to the '90s: to the people who
got us into war, dirtied our history and killed our neighbours, back in a
different, almost as evil form. Rule of crime, rule of money, rule of cheap,
rule of people who do not know how to be ashamed.

That night Ćacilend, their hooligan camp outside the Assembly, was torn down —
people walked off with its chairs as souvenirs.

End of lies, end of blackmail, end of embarrassment, end of fear.
**Start of freedom.**
